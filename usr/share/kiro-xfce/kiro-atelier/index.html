What it does

Right-click, paint

Once Kiro Atelier is installed, right-click pictures in Thunar and choose Paint with Kiro Atelier. Or drop a picture or a whole folder on it: it paints them one after another.

13 ready-made styles

Every one is shown below on the same photos. Make your own profiles on top, or paint one picture at several strengths and keep the one you like.

Named and ready

Each painting gets a name that says what is in it, and becomes your wallpaper with one click.

One painter for the house

A computer without a suitable graphics card can send its pictures to another computer in your home that has one, and get the paintings back.

What your computer needs

Graphics card

  • NVIDIA RTX 20xx or newer (RTX 20, 30, 40, 50 series)
  • at least 6 GB of video memory
  • GTX 16xx cards work, but are untested and much slower
  • AMD and Intel graphics are not supported

The rest

  • 16 GB of memory (12 GB runs, with swapping)
  • about 20 GB free disk space (ComfyUI and the painting models)
  • an Arch-based Linux system: Kiro, KiroTux or Arch

Check first, for free

KA Check tells you in a minute whether this computer can paint, and what to do when it can't. It changes nothing:

sudo pacman -S kiro-atelier-check

The photos

Every profile below starts from these three photos.

Default

strength 0.65 · impasto · 3840 × 2160

The original Kiro Atelier look: thick impasto, a good balance between photo and paint.

Palette Knife

strength 0.75 · impasto · 3840 × 2160 · detail pass

Loose, heavy impasto with real knife ridges; the photo shows through less. Twice as slow.

Gentle Touch

strength 0.45 · impasto · 3840 × 2160

Stays close to the photo: same shapes and light, with a soft layer of paint over it.

Gallery Print

strength 0.55 · impasto · 3840 × 2160 · detail pass · PNG

Best quality for printing or a big screen: extra detail pass, saved as PNG. Slow, large files.

Morning Wash

strength 0.60 · watercolour · 3840 × 2160

Soft watercolour: light washes, gentle edges, paper texture.

Monsoon

strength 0.75 · watercolour · 3840 × 2160

Wild watercolour: colours run and bleed into each other, far from the photo.

Poster Studio

strength 0.70 · gouache · 3840 × 2160

Gouache: flat, bold colour shapes like a travel poster.

Pastel Light

strength 0.60 · pastel · 3840 × 2160

Soft pastel: chalky strokes and blended light on toned paper.

Floating World

strength 0.55 · ukiyo-e · 3840 × 2160

Japanese woodblock print: clean outlines, flat colours, patterned waves and clouds. People and action scenes stay recognisable.

Edo Landscape

strength 0.65 · ukiyo-e · 3840 × 2160

Strongest woodblock look, for landscapes. People and action scenes get replaced.

Linocut

strength 0.70 · woodcut · 3840 × 2160

Woodcut print: bold carved black lines and a few flat colours. Best on landscapes.

Charcoal Sketch

strength 0.65 · charcoal · 3840 × 2160

Charcoal drawing: smudged black and grey strokes on rough paper, strong contrast.

Light Files

strength 0.60 · impasto · 1920 × 1080

Impasto at 1920 × 1080: small files for a laptop or a second screen. Paints as fast as 4K.
